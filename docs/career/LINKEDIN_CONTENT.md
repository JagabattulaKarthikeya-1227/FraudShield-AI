## Template 1: The Engineering Deep-Dive (Recommended)

I'm incredibly proud to announce the completion of my latest project: **FraudShield AI** 🚀

When dealing with credit card fraud, you face two massive engineering hurdles: 
1. The data is heavily imbalanced (0.17% fraud).
2. Financial regulations require you to explain *why* an AI made a decision (no Black Boxes).

I built FraudShield AI as a full-stack, end-to-end demonstration of how to solve this at an enterprise level. 

🛠️ **The Architecture:**
- **The ML Engine**: A Stacking Ensemble (Extra Trees + MLP) optimized specifically for PR-AUC, using SMOTE strictly in cross-validation to prevent data leakage.
- **Explainable AI (XAI)**: Integrated KernelSHAP to mathematically prove feature importance for every blocked transaction.
- **The UI**: A luxury React/Framer Motion frontend featuring a live SRE dashboard and 3D WebGL data visualizations.
- **DevSecOps**: Built with Zero-Trust principles, utilizing Argon2id hashing and HttpOnly JWT cookies to mitigate OWASP Top 10 threats.

This project was a masterclass in bridging the gap between Data Science and Production Software Engineering.
